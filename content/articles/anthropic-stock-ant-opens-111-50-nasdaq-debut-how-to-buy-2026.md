Anthropic's stock is officially trading, and it jumped right out of the gate. Shares of the Claude maker opened on the Nasdaq at **$111.50** under the ticker **ANT** shortly before noon Eastern on Thursday, about **34% above** the $83 IPO price, [CNN reported](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/19/business/live-news/anthropic-ipo).

The first trade pushed Anthropic's value past **$2 trillion**, according to [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/live/2026/11/19/technology/anthropic-ipo-ai), up from about $1.5 trillion at the IPO price and more than double what the company was worth six months ago.

## Update, Nov. 20: Anthropic opens at $89.86 and slides toward its $83 IPO price

Anthropic's second day of regular trading started lower. ANT opened at **$89.86** at 9:30 a.m. ET on Friday, down 7.5% from Thursday's $97.17 close, [Fox News reported](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/anthropic-stock-ipo-compute-costs-to-rise-11-20-26). In the first hour it traded between **$85.67 and $92.56** and was down about **11%** at roughly $86, according to [Yahoo Finance data](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/ANT/). That leaves the stock only about $3 above the $83 IPO price and about 23% below Thursday's $111.50 first trade.

The selling spread across Wall Street. Anthropic shares fell more than 9% at the opening bell, and commentary quoted in [The Guardian's live coverage](https://www.theguardian.com/business/live/2026/nov/20/asia-stocks-worst-anthropic-ipo-ai-stock-market-latest-updates-news) called the $83 IPO price "the level to watch," warning that a deeper selloff could put the stock at risk of dipping below it. About 139 million shares had changed hands by 10:16 a.m. ET, [MLQ.ai reported](https://mlq.ai/news/anthropic-shares-fall-toward-their-83-ipo-price-on-day-two-as-the-greenshoe-lifts-the-raise-to-935-billion/) using StockAnalysis data.

Even after the drop, Anthropic was worth about $1.14 trillion by intraday market value, Yahoo Finance data show. The IPO itself grew on Friday to about $93.5 billion after underwriters bought extra shares, as detailed below.

## Update, Nov. 20: Anthropic stock falls about 10% before the bell on day two

Anthropic shares are sliding on their second day of trading. ANT was down about **10%** in premarket trading on Friday morning, at around **$85.74** as of 8:30 a.m. ET, according to [Yahoo Finance data](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/ANT/). That is down from Thursday's $97.17 close but still above the $83 IPO price. Earlier in the morning the drop was closer to 6%, and it deepened as the selloff in AI stocks spread, [Reuters reported](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/us-stocks/wall-street-guide/anthropic-shares-fall-10-post-stellar-debut-delivers-reality-check-for-ai-trade/articleshow/136027823.cms).

The pressure is broad. Nasdaq 100 futures fell about 1.6% before dawn, [according to Reuters](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/inside-the-market/market-news/article-us-futures-fall-after-sp-500-drops-for-six-straight-sessions-asia-set/). Nvidia slipped 2.6% in premarket trading, while AMD, Broadcom, Marvell and Micron lost between 3.5% and 5.5%. Amazon, Meta and Microsoft each fell more than 1.8%, a notable move since Amazon and Alphabet are among Anthropic's biggest investors. Wall Street's main indexes are on track for their steepest weekly decline since April 2025.

Investors are taking profits after the first-day pop and questioning whether Anthropic's valuation can hold. [Reuters noted](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/article-anthropics-post-debut-stumble-delivers-reality-check-for-ai-trade/) that the stock trades at about 40 times its sales over the past 12 months. Hawkish signals from Federal Reserve officials and a Bloomberg report that Apple is building its own AI agent also weighed on sentiment.

"A decline in share value is normal following a high-profile listing, but we don't know if that decline will be in line with other IPOs or more volatile, and that should raise alarms for investors," Tejal Patel, executive director at the SOC Investment Group, told Reuters.

The IPO also got bigger on Friday. Anthropic's underwriters exercised their option to sell additional shares, lifting the money raised in the IPO to about **$93.5 billion**, or about $119.5 billion including the $26 billion private placement, Reuters reported. Some analysts see support ahead: index-tracking funds could buy up to 30% of the company's shares by year-end, according to an estimate from the analytics firm Intropic cited by Reuters.

Wall Street analysts are mostly upbeat. Melius Research started coverage with a Buy rating and a $155 price target, and Needham rated the stock a Buy with a $150 target, [Invezz reported](https://finwire.io/news/stock-markets-news/anthropic-slides-over-9-in-premarket-after-blockbuster-debut-whats-going-on). Retail traders bought and sold about $772 million of Anthropic shares on debut day, the most for any IPO since Vanda Research began tracking the data in 2012.

Regular trading opens at **9:30 a.m. ET**, and premarket prices often change once it does. Anthropic later opened lower; see the update above.

## Update: Anthropic closes first day at $97.17

Anthropic finished its first day of trading at **$97.17**, up **17.1%** from the $83 IPO price, according to Nasdaq data reported by [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/live/anthropic-ipo-live-company-said-to-price-stock-at-83-begin-trading-thursday-212656452.html) and [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/fionariley/2026/11/19/anthropic-ipo-live-stock-closes-day-at-97-up-more-than-17/).

The stock hit a high of **$115.10** shortly after its first trade, then slid through the afternoon to a low of **$92.72** before recovering into the close. The closing price is about 13% below the $111.50 opening trade. Roughly 840 million shares changed hands.

At the close, Forbes put Anthropic's market value at about **$1.27 trillion**, based on shares outstanding. On a fully diluted basis, which counts stock options and other securities, the company is worth closer to $1.76 trillion.

## Anthropic stock: the key numbers

Anthropic trades as **ANT** on the Nasdaq Global Select Market. It [set its IPO price at $83](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/18/anthropic-share-price-stock-market-ipo) on Wednesday night, above its $70 to $78 range, and sold 980 million shares, up from the 825 million it originally planned.

That raised about **$81.3 billion**, the largest IPO on record. After underwriters bought extra shares on Friday, the IPO total rose to about $93.5 billion, or about $119.5 billion including a separate $26 billion private placement. The deal beat the $75 billion that SpaceX raised in June to become the biggest IPO ever by money raised, though SpaceX debuted at a higher valuation of about $1.77 trillion.

## Why trading started hours after the opening bell

CEO Dario Amodei and his sister, president Daniela Amodei, rang the Nasdaq opening bell at 9:30 a.m. ET. But the stock did not trade until much later.

That is normal for a big IPO. Nasdaq first runs an opening auction that collects buy and sell orders and finds a price that balances them. Ahead of the first trade, the indicated price climbed steadily, from about $105 in the morning to around $110 just before trading began.

## How to buy Anthropic stock

Now that ANT is listed, anyone with a regular brokerage account can buy it. Robinhood, Fidelity, Charles Schwab, E*Trade, Vanguard and most other major U.S. brokers list Nasdaq stocks. Search for the ticker "ANT" and make sure it shows Anthropic, PBC.

You do not need $100 or more to get started, because many brokers let you buy fractional shares. New listings can swing sharply in minutes, so many investors use a limit order, which sets the highest price you are willing to pay and keeps you from being filled at a sudden spike.

Financial advisers interviewed ahead of the debut said there is no rush to buy in the first minutes, since prices often settle after the first few days of trading. Options on ANT are expected to start trading as soon as Monday, Nov. 23.

The shares that trade as ANT are Class A stock. Anthropic also sold separate non-voting Class N shares in its private placement, and those do not trade on the Nasdaq.

## What investors are buying

Anthropic, founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers, makes the Claude AI models and the Claude Code programming tool. Its revenue has exploded, from $386 million in 2024 to $4.6 billion in 2025 and $16.3 billion in just the first half of 2026, according to its prospectus.

It is also spending enormous sums. The company reported a large loss for the first half of 2026 and disclosed more than $450 billion in computing commitments through 2030.

Anthropic is the first company at the front of the generative AI boom to go public. Rival OpenAI has delayed its own IPO. That makes Anthropic's trading a closely watched test of investor appetite for AI, at a time when worries about the cost of the AI buildout have pushed tech stocks sharply lower this month.
