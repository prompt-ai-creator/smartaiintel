Joe Burrow passed for 409 yards and four touchdowns, Ja'Marr Chase caught 11 passes for 231 yards, and the Cincinnati Bengals routed the Pittsburgh Steelers 38-18 on Sunday night to take sole possession of first place in the AFC North.

The win was Cincinnati's fifth in a row and lifted the Bengals to 7-2. Pittsburgh, coming off a bye week, fell to 6-3.

## Burrow's first-half blitz

Burrow was close to perfect before halftime. He completed 26 of 34 passes for 302 yards and three touchdowns in the first half, only the second time in his career he had passed 300 yards before the break, according to the Associated Press.

The Bengals scored on four of their five first-half drives and led 24-3 at the break. The only empty possession came when Evan McPherson missed a 43-yard field goal wide right, ending his run of 25 straight successful kicks.

Burrow finished 35 of 44 for 409 yards with no interceptions and a passer rating of 135.7. He was sacked only once.

## Chase runs wild

Chase was the night's other star. He caught 11 of the 14 passes thrown his way for 231 yards, including a 53-yard gain, and repeatedly beat Pittsburgh's secondary deep.

Running back Chase Brown scored twice, both on short touchdown catches, and finished with 82 yards from scrimmage. Tee Higgins added five catches for 66 yards.

## Steelers shut down

The Steelers never found a rhythm. Aaron Rodgers completed 15 of 29 passes for 143 yards and two touchdowns, both after the game was decided, and was sacked three times.

Pittsburgh's running game, which beat Cincinnati in Week 3, was held in check. Jaylen Warren, who ran for 127 yards in the Steelers' 30-27 win over the Bengals in September, managed 15 yards on seven carries.

Cincinnati outgained Pittsburgh 484 yards to 222 and had 30 first downs to the Steelers' 17.

The Bengals had played in Europe the week before. According to the AP, it was the first time in four opportunities that a team that played in Europe the week before had beaten an opponent coming off a bye.

## What's next

With the victory, the Bengals moved a game clear of Pittsburgh in the AFC North and avenged their 30-27 loss in Week 3, splitting the season series between the rivals.
