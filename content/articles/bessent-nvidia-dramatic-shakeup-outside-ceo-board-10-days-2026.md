Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has called for a "dramatic shakeup" at Nvidia, saying the world's most valuable chipmaker should look outside its own ranks for a new chief executive after the [resignation of Jensen Huang](https://smartaiintel.com/articles/jensen-huang-resigns-nvidia-ceo-colette-kress-harvey-jones-601b-wipeout/).

Speaking on Fox News' *Sunday Morning Futures*, Bessent openly questioned the company's culture and asked whether it chased sales that may have broken US export rules.

"This makes me think that as the board conducts a search for a new leader that they need to go outside the company," he said. "They need a dramatic shakeup."

## "Seemingly illicit sales"

Bessent focused on how Nvidia's revenue from Singapore climbed even as its reported China sales fell. US investigators suspect that Singapore-based data-center firm Megaspeed helped move advanced Nvidia chips into China.

"I want to keep coming back to what was the culture at Nvidia, and were they pressing these seemingly illicit sales to goose their revenues and their earnings?" he said.

He said he wants "answers in the next 10 days from the board and from the outside counsel". Nvidia's board [hired law firm Wachtell Lipton to run an internal probe](https://smartaiintel.com/articles/nvidia-rebounds-wachtell-lipton-probe-trump-defends-jensen-huang/) last week.

Bessent also compared Nvidia with the banking sector, which he said would face much tougher treatment. "If this were a bank," he said, the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency "would be in there right now, and a lot of the executives would be removed."

## A pointed message for the board

The remarks raise the stakes for Nvidia's succession search. The board has been led since Thursday by chairman Harvey C. Jones, with chief financial officer Colette Kress serving as interim CEO. [Who Kress and Jones are, and how they got here](https://smartaiintel.com/articles/who-are-colette-kress-harvey-jones-nvidia-interim-ceo-chairman/), has become a central question for investors.

The Financial Times reported on Friday that the board had [approached Arm CEO Rene Haas](https://smartaiintel.com/articles/nvidia-approaches-arm-ceo-rene-haas-succeed-jensen-huang/) about the job. Haas would count as an outside hire, though he spent several years at Nvidia earlier in his career.

Bessent's comments make it harder for the board to choose a long-serving insider without facing criticism from Washington.

## Why Huang left

Huang, who co-founded Nvidia in 1993 and led it for 33 years, resigned on Thursday after reports about a relationship with Alice Huang, a former executive at Megaspeed whom US officials suspect of working for Chinese intelligence. Megaspeed says she left the company in August 2025. The [FBI is investigating](https://smartaiintel.com/articles/fbi-probe-shareholder-lawsuit-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang/), and shareholders have filed a lawsuit.

Not everyone close to the administration wants a clean break. Elon Musk said on Saturday that Nvidia will lose "critical" deals without Huang. President Trump has [defended Huang](https://smartaiintel.com/articles/nvidia-rebounds-wachtell-lipton-probe-trump-defends-jensen-huang/) in recent days.

## Tension with Commerce

Bessent's comments also point to a split inside the administration. Axios reported that he confronted Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick in the White House Situation Room over why export restrictions on Megaspeed had not been enforced. Commerce has denied that account. SmartAI News [reported last week](https://smartaiintel.com/articles/howard-lutnick-stalls-trump-nvidia-china-chip-crackdown-bessent-musk/) on how Lutnick delayed a crackdown on Nvidia chip sales to China several times.

Asked who was responsible for the export rules, Bessent said simply: "Commerce had the pen."

The New York Times has reported that Lutnick pushed to loosen chip rules in ways that overlapped with his family's business interests. Cantor Fitzgerald, the firm Lutnick used to run, cut its Nvidia holdings by nearly half, mostly through client swaps.

The policy backdrop is complicated. In December, Trump allowed sales of Nvidia's H200 chips to approved Chinese buyers, with the US government taking a 25% cut. Chips more powerful than the H200 remain banned.

## Earnings on Tuesday

Bessent's intervention lands two days before Nvidia reports quarterly results on Tuesday, November 17. It will be the company's first earnings call without Huang as CEO, and analysts expect questions about the Singapore figures Bessent raised.

Nvidia shares [swung sharply last week](https://smartaiintel.com/articles/nvidia-reclaims-4-trillion-close-third-jensen-huang-affair-paul-weiss/) as the scandal unfolded, briefly losing and then regaining a $4 trillion market value.
