Bitcoin has fallen below **$60,000** for the first time since early July, as Friday's selloff in tech and AI stocks spread into crypto.

The world's largest cryptocurrency slipped under the level shortly before 11 a.m. ET on Friday, Nov. 20. It was down 3.08% at $59,610 when Reuters first reported the break, saying lofty valuation concerns weighing on the AI trade and other risky assets were behind the drop, [according to CNA](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/bitcoin-falls-below-60000-level-6570016). A little after 11 a.m. ET, bitcoin was around **$59,300**, down about 6% in 24 hours, after touching about $59,000, [CoinGecko data](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/bitcoin) show. Ether, the second-largest cryptocurrency, fell more than 7% to about $1,650.

## A fast drop through a key level

The break came with little warning. Bitcoin traded near $61,400 at 9:40 a.m. ET and slid to about $59,600 by 10:45 a.m., a drop of nearly 3% in about an hour, [Bitcoin Magazine reported](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/markets/bitcoin-price-crashes-below-60000). It had held above $61,000 for most of the morning.

Many traders saw $60,000 as a floor. Katie Stockton of Fairlead Strategies had told CNBC the bottom of bitcoin's trading range sits "just below 60,000." [CNBC said](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) bitcoin was now heading for a retest of its July low of about $57,737, while [FXStreet noted](https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/breaking-bitcoin-slips-below-60-000-as-selling-pressure-accelerates-202611201542) the yearly low near $57,800 as the next target if selling continues. Bitcoin is down nearly 8.5% this week.

## Why bitcoin is falling

The crypto slide is tied to a wider retreat from risky assets. AI and chip stocks fell for another day on Friday, with Anthropic among the biggest names under pressure. The Claude maker's shares [slid toward their $83 IPO price](/articles/anthropic-stock-ant-opens-111-50-nasdaq-debut-how-to-buy-2026/) on their second day of trading, just a day after a record debut.

Some in the market have pointed to investors selling assets like bitcoin to raise cash for the Anthropic offering, [The Crypto Times reported](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/11/20/bitcoin-price-today-btc-drops-to-61k-hitting-lowest-level-since-july/), though others see that as a smaller factor. The same report pointed to heavy outflows from spot bitcoin ETFs, expectations that the Federal Reserve could keep rates higher, a stronger dollar and lingering damage from a major exchange hack this year.

Leveraged traders have been hit hard. More than $1 billion in long positions, or bets on higher prices, has been wiped out as bitcoin slid, [CryptoPotato reported](https://cryptopotato.com/over-1-billion-in-longs-wrecked-as-bitcoin-btc-slides-below-60k/). Bitcoin has lost more than $21,000 in just over two weeks.

## How we got here

The slump has been building for weeks. Bitcoin [fell below $69,000 on Nov. 13](/articles/bitcoin-plunges-below-69000-ethereum-under-2000-treasury-yields/) as Treasury yields climbed and a [tech selloff](/articles/nasdaq-tumbles-nvidia-reverses-lower-chip-selloff-traps-dip-buyers/) hit chip stocks. Early Friday, it sank to about $61,300 as a stock rout spread across Asia, with Japan's Nikkei and South Korea's KOSPI falling sharply, [Bitcoin.com News reported](https://news.bitcoin.com/market-updates/bitcoin-price-lowest-since-july-asian-stocks-crash-kospi/).

Bitcoin set its record of about $126,000 in October 2025. At around $59,000, it has lost more than half of its value from that peak. CoinShares head of research James Butterfill called the drop a "continuation of the existing bear market," Bitcoin Magazine reported, with a base case of trading between $60,000 and $90,000 through much of 2027.

## What to watch next

Traders are now watching whether bitcoin can climb back above $60,000 or falls toward its July low near $57,700. Crypto trades around the clock, so prices could move sharply over the weekend, when stock markets are closed.
