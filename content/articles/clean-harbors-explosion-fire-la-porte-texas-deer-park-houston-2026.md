An explosion and fire at a hazardous waste incineration plant in La Porte, Texas, shook homes and rattled windows across the eastern Houston area on Tuesday night. Officials say the fire has been put out and no one was reported hurt.

The blast happened at about 8:48 p.m. local time at the Clean Harbors Deer Park Incineration Facility, at 2027 Independence Parkway South in La Porte, about 25 miles east of Houston.

In a notice posted shortly before 9 p.m. through the area's Community Awareness Emergency Response (CAER) system, Clean Harbors reported an "explosion, fire, Process/Unit upset" at the site. It warned that "emergency vehicles, flames, noise, sirens, smoke, activity may be noticeable to the community."

"We are actively responding to the incident. Protecting employees, neighboring facilities, the community, and the environment remains a priority," the company said.

## Residents report a loud boom and shaking

Many people in La Porte and nearby Deer Park said on social media that they heard a large bang and felt their homes shake. Some said windows rattled miles from the plant.

"We are aware of reports from residents experiencing loud noises and vibrations associated with the incident," the La Porte Office of Emergency Management said. "At this time, no actions are needed from La Porte residents."

The City of Deer Park said it was monitoring reports of a possible tank fire at the facility. The City of Pasadena also said its residents did not need to take any action.

## Fire out, no hazardous readings

The City of La Porte later said responders had put out the fire. Officials said the wind was carrying smoke away from homes, and residents did not need to seek shelter.

"The City of Deer Park's air monitoring system is not detecting any hazardous readings," the city said. "Per Clean Harbors' recommendation, it is not advised to shelter in place at this time."

No injuries or evacuations have been reported.

## Cause under investigation

Officials have not said what caused the explosion, or what materials were involved. Clean Harbors said it will post another update through CAER once the incident is resolved.

The Deer Park facility handles hazardous and industrial waste, including liquids, solids, sludge and gases, under permits issued under the federal Resource Conservation and Recovery Act and the Toxic Substances Control Act, according to the company.

La Porte sits along the Houston Ship Channel, one of the largest petrochemical hubs in the United States. In September 2024, a pipeline fire in La Porte burned for four days after a vehicle crashed through a fence and struck a valve.
