The NFC East-leading New York Giants host division rival Washington Commanders at MetLife Stadium on Thursday night (`8:15 p.m. ET`, Prime Video) to open NFL Week 10, with late-week injury updates centering on Giants quarterback J.J. McCarthy's bruised hip, running back Cam Skattebo's return from a two-game absence, and a forced interior-line shakeup in front of Commanders quarterback Jayden Daniels.

Exactly one month after the two rivals played to a chaotic 27–27 overtime tie on October 11, head coach John Harbaugh's Giants (`5–2–1`) enter Thursday's "White Out" home game holding a two-game cushion atop the NFC East and listed as slim 1.5-point favorites (`43`–`43.5` over/under) as they chase their first home victory over Washington since Week 7 of the 2023 season.

## Giants injury report: J.J. McCarthy cleared to start as Cam Skattebo returns

McCarthy moved in and out of the huddle during New York's gritty 19–11 Week 9 victory over the Philadelphia Eagles after absorbing a painful hip pointer, raising early-week questions on a short four-day turnaround. According to NBC Sports' final Week 10 injury update, however, McCarthy is set to start Thursday night behind top perimeter target Malik Nabers and tight end Isaiah Likely.

New York's ground game receives a timely reinforcement with Skattebo projected back in uniform after missing the past two weeks with a hamstring strain. Despite the layoff, Skattebo still paces the Giants' backfield with 418 rushing yards on 98 carries (`4.3` yards per attempt) — highlighted by a 17-carry, 124-yard breakout against the Houston Texans in Week 7 — giving Harbaugh a physical counterbalance to ease pressure on McCarthy's hip in cold East Rutherford conditions.

Defensively, New York rides surging rookie pass-rush and linebacker play led by Abdul Carter and Arvell Reese under coordinator Dennard Wilson, whose interior blitz packages suffocated Philadelphia last Sunday.

## Commanders injury report: Lucas Patrick out with concussion, Treylon Burks active

Washington enters Week 10 in what Daniels has framed publicly as a must-win divisional test to keep the Commanders' postseason hopes intact, but Daniels will operate behind a reshuffled offensive line. Starting center Lucas Patrick suffered a concussion in Sunday's outing and has been ruled out on the short week, forcing Nick Allegretti back to the pivot directly opposite Wilson's A-gap pressure looks.

At wide receiver, Treylon Burks has been cleared to return for Thursday Night Football after sitting out Week 9 with a foot injury, though team observers cautioned his snap count could be managed after logging only a single limited practice session Tuesday, leaving Antonio Williams in a rotational role.

On the defensive side, Washington coordinator Daronte Jones and senior defensive adviser Steve Wilks welcome reinforcements in the secondary aimed at limiting explosive plays from Nabers after surrendering 27 points in last month's overtime stalemate, setting up one of the tightest divisional spreads on the [midseason NFL slate](https://smartaiintel.com/articles/vikings-trade-kirk-cousins-49ers-keenan-allen-pat-murphy-mlb-history/).
