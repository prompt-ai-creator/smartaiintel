## Why the communications role matters

A press secretary’s work extends beyond delivering an effective television appearance. The role requires communicating administration positions, handling detailed questions and maintaining a usable public record across subjects ranging from domestic policy to international crises. The central test is not simply whether a spokesperson can attract attention, but whether their answers provide dependable information.

AP places the proposal within a wider personnel transition. It reports that Trump announced Stephen Miller would replace Susie Wiles as chief of staff following Republican losses in the midterm elections. AP also describes the press operation as unsettled following Katie Zacharia’s brief tenure. These developments provide context; they do not prove a specific motive for the offer to White. White’s relationship with Trump is established rather than newly formed.
