Elon Musk, who once told a cheering Trump rally that he was "dark gothic MAGA," now says he is not MAGA at all.

"I'm not MAGA," Musk said on Wednesday in a conversation with Andrew Ross Sorkin at the New York Times DealBook Summit. "I'm not, like, sure what MAGA means," he added, "but if that means sort of blind adherence to a set of policies by the Republican Party, then I certainly don't have that. I think there are positives and negatives in both parties."

The comments were Musk's most detailed reaction yet to a midterm election in which his record spending failed to keep Republicans in power, The New York Times reported.

## 'Pretty foolish' about $500 million

Musk spent nearly $500 million backing Republican candidates and causes this election cycle, the largest amount ever spent by a non-candidate in a single US election, according to The New York Times. That included $168 million to the Senate Leadership Fund, the main Republican Senate super PAC, and $30 million to the Congressional Leadership Fund, which backs House Republicans, according to Quartz.

It did not work. Democrats won control of both chambers of Congress. [House Democrats have nominated Hakeem Jeffries](https://smartaiintel.com/articles/hakeem-jeffries-nominated-house-speaker-first-black-speaker-251-11-vote-2026/) to become speaker, and [Mary Peltola's win in Alaska](https://smartaiintel.com/articles/mary-peltola-wins-alaska-senate-beats-dan-sullivan-democrats-56-seats-2026/) gave Democrats 56 Senate seats.

"The Republicans were routed in this election," Musk said. "I feel pretty foolish about having spent so much money and not seeing much for it."

"You can put a lot of money into an election and have an effect that is relatively minor. And I think that was the case here," he said. "Probably if I could rewind the clock, I would have spent less money."

Asked about backing losing candidates such as Ken Paxton in the Texas Senate race, Musk said his political push "failed, by the way, obviously," according to The New York Times.

## From 'dark gothic MAGA' to 'not MAGA'

The comments mark a sharp change from October 2024, when Musk joined Donald Trump on stage at a rally at Madison Square Garden. "I'm not just MAGA. I'm dark gothic MAGA," he told the crowd.

Musk went on to spend more than $250 million helping Trump and Republicans win in 2024. He later led the administration's cost-cutting effort.

In May 2025, Musk said: "If I see a reason to do political spending in the future, I will do it. I do not currently see a reason." He went on to spend nearly twice as much in the midterms.

Critics online were quick to point out the change. Clips of Musk's 2024 "dark gothic MAGA" moment spread again within hours of his remarks.

## A softer tone on Democrats

Musk, who lives in Texas, also struck an optimistic note about the state's incoming Democratic governor, Gina Hinojosa, The New York Times reported. Hinojosa supports continuing a freeze on new data center construction.

The Times said Musk's shift reflects a wider effort by business leaders to adjust to a new political landscape, as Democrats prepare to take power and target billionaires and big tech projects such as data centers.

## Sharp words on Ukraine

Musk took a harder line when asked about limits on Ukraine's military use of his Starlink satellite network.

He said Starlink was not designed for offensive military operations. "Do not ask me as a private individual to make kill decisions that sometimes result in the loss of innocent lives," Musk said.

Asked about Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky's statements that his troops use a military-grade version of Starlink for offensive strikes, Musk said: "He's not always truthful."
