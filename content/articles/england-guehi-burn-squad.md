Dan Burn has been called into the England squad after Marc Guéhi withdrew through injury, forcing Thomas Tuchel to adjust his defensive options before the final Nations League matches of the year.

The Newcastle United defender joined the squad at Tottenham Hotspur’s training ground on Monday, England confirmed. Guéhi will remain with Manchester City after being substituted at the interval during Saturday’s match at Nottingham Forest.

The [official squad update confirming Burn’s call up](https://www.englandfootball.com/articles/2026/Nov/09/england-mens-squads-updates-november-international-window-2026) does not specify the injury or give a recovery timetable. His withdrawal therefore confirms that he will miss this camp, not how long he will be unavailable to his club.

## An experienced option for Tuchel

Burn arrives with 11 England caps rather than as a newcomer to international football. His inclusion restores an option in defence, although the call up does not establish that he will start or take over Guéhi’s exact role.

Tuchel must now decide how to use the defenders available to him as England prepare to face Croatia and Spain. The squad change is confirmed; the lineup and any resulting tactical adjustment are not.

That distinction matters because an injury replacement can alter the choices available to a manager without determining the team he eventually selects.

## Qualification remains the immediate target

England’s [squad announcement for the November fixtures](https://www.englandfootball.com/articles/2026/Nov/06/england-senior-mens-squad-announcement-for-croatia-spain-games-20261106) says the team is seeking a place in the Nations League quarter finals by finishing in the top two of its group.

The same announcement says avoiding defeat against Croatia would secure qualification. That makes the first match the immediate competitive test, before attention turns to Spain.

For Guéhi, the next update will need to come from his club or another confirmed medical assessment. For Burn, Monday’s call up gives him another opportunity to contribute during the international window, with selection still Tuchel’s decision.
