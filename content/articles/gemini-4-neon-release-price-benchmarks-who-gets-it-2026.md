Google has released **Gemini 4 Neon**, a new model that sits between its fast everyday model, Gemini 4 Hydrogen, and its most powerful model, Gemini 4 Carbon. Google says Neon delivers close to Carbon-level results on coding, reasoning and agent tasks, but faster and at a lower price.

The model went live on Wednesday, Nov. 18, for paying Gemini subscribers and developers. It arrived less than a week after Google launched [Gemini 4 Hydrogen](https://smartaiintel.com/articles/google-launches-gemini-4-hydrogen-search-api-coding-agents/) across Search, the Gemini app and the API.

## Gemini 4 Neon release date and who gets it

Google announced Neon on Wednesday in a post by Koray Kavukcuoglu, senior vice president at Google DeepMind and Google's chief AI architect. It launched as a stable, generally available model on day one rather than as a preview.

Neon is rolling out to:

- **Gemini app:** Google AI Pro and Google AI Ultra subscribers on web and mobile
- **Google Antigravity:** paid AI Pro and Ultra subscribers using Google's agent coding tool
- **Developers:** the paid Gemini API through Google AI Studio and Gemini for business

Free Gemini users do not get Neon. They continue to use Hydrogen.

## Neon replaces Argon and Gemini 3.1 Pro

The launch reshuffles the Gemini app's model picker. According to 9to5Google, Neon replaces Gemini 4 Argon, and the long-running Gemini 3.1 Pro is finally retired. Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite now powers the "Auto" option for paid users.

The picker now offers four choices:

- **Auto:** picks a model for you
- **Everyday help:** Gemini 4 Hydrogen
- **Deeper analysis:** Gemini 4 Neon
- **Code and complex work:** Gemini 4 Carbon

Google says Neon is built for "complex coding, analytical reasoning, and production-grade enterprise workflows." Carbon remains the choice for the hardest, longest projects, while Hydrogen handles quick everyday requests.

## Gemini 4 Neon price

On the paid Gemini API, Neon costs:

- **$3** per million input tokens
- **$0.15** per million cached input tokens, a 95% discount
- **$15** per million output tokens

That is double Hydrogen's $1.50 input and $7.50 output price. Argon and Carbon are on promotional pricing of $2 and $10 until Nov. 30. After that, Carbon rises to $4 and $20, making Neon about 25% cheaper than Carbon.

Like Hydrogen, Neon has a 1 million-token context window and four thinking levels: low, medium, high and extra high.

## Gemini 4 Neon benchmarks

Google published a long list of test results. The headline numbers:

- **Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index:** 61 at its highest thinking level, ranking third overall behind larger, slower models
- **Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index:** 58.6, ranking first
- **Terminal-Bench 4.1:** 65.2%, up from Hydrogen's 57%
- **Vals Index:** second place at 70.9%, and first on both the Legal Research Bench and Tax Agent Bench
- **Agent Arena Index:** second globally with a score of 1,336 across more than 4,200 real agent sessions
- **Science and math:** best in class on Terminal-Bench Science (70.5%), RiemannBench (84%) and HLE-Diamond (71.3%)
- **Business simulations:** first on Zapier's AutomationBench (56.3%) and Andon Labs' Vending-Bench 2 ($16,843)

Google also said Neon recorded its lowest rate of made-up answers yet in internal Antigravity testing.

Early business testers backed up the speed claim. Glean's AI product lead, Thai Tran, said that in early tests Neon "matched or exceeded Gemini 4 Carbon on overall quality at 3x the speed and 60% of the cost."

## What Neon changes in the Gemini app

For everyday subscribers, Google says Neon writes in a clearer, more direct tone and reads spreadsheets, PDFs and photos more precisely. It pulls more details from the web for fast-moving topics, such as comparing prices before a big purchase.

Gemini's agent-style "Tasks" can now run on Neon. Google says this lets multi-step jobs, like checking billing statements or cross-checking receipts for a refund, finish faster while using less of a subscriber's usage limit.

According to Google's model card, Neon is adapted from Carbon and has a knowledge cutoff of March 2026.

## A fast pace of Gemini 4 releases

Neon is the fourth Gemini 4 model in about five weeks. Argon came first in October, Carbon followed on Nov. 10 and [Hydrogen arrived on Nov. 12](https://smartaiintel.com/articles/google-launches-gemini-4-hydrogen-search-api-coding-agents/).

Readers are already struggling to keep up with the names. In 9to5Google's comments, one popular reply said it was "getting difficult to keep up with all these names" compared with the old Flash and Pro labels.
