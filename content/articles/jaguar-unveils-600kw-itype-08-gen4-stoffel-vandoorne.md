Reigning Formula E teams' champion Jaguar TCS Racing pulled the covers off its striking blue and white I Type 08 single seater in London on Tuesday, launching the British manufacturer's bid for the championship's transformative GEN4 era with permanent all wheel drive, peak output of 600 kilowatts, and a dual world champion driver pairing of Stoffel Vandoorne and Antonio Felix da Costa. Revealed at Jaguar's Knightsbridge showcase on November 10 according to [WhichEV](https://www.whichev.net/2026/11/10/jaguar-reveals-600kw-i-type-08-as-vandoorne-joins-da-costa-for-gen4/), the new Season 13 challenger heads straight to official pre season testing at Madrid's Jarama circuit from November 16 through November 20.

Promoting Vandoorne from test and reserve duties into a full time race seat completes one of the most accomplished driver lineups in electric motorsport history. Replacing long serving Jaguar stalwart Mitch Evans following Evans's departure to Opel, the 2022 Formula E champion Vandoorne partners 2020 champion da Costa to give the factory squad two title winners boasting a combined 18 career victories and 49 podium finishes as the 2026 to 2027 campaign approaches.

Visually, Tuesday's Knightsbridge presentation marked a dramatic departure from the monochrome black and white liveries that defined Jaguar's GEN3 cars. Returning to heritage shades of vivid lightning blue paired with crisp white bodywork, the I Type 08 underscores Jaguar's push to link track proven electric powertrain engineering directly to its next generation of road going luxury electric vehicles.

## Inside the 600kW permanent all wheel drive GEN4 technical leap

Season 13 ushers in the largest performance jump in Formula E's thirteen year existence, elevating electric open wheel racing closer to traditional grand prix cornering and acceleration benchmarks at the very moment [Formula 1 manufacturers are navigating strict 2027 hybrid homologation limits](https://grandepremio.com/en/f1/fia-denies-extra-f1-engine-upgrades-after-final-2026-review-as-mercedes-ferrari-retain-allowances/). At the heart of the Jaguar I Type 08 sits a twin axle architecture pairing a 350 kilowatt rear powertrain with a 250 kilowatt front powertrain to deliver 600 kilowatts of peak propulsion, equivalent to more than 800 brake horsepower.

Under FIA GEN4 sporting rules, drivers deploy 450 kilowatts in standard race trim before unlocking the full 600 kilowatt ceiling during qualifying duels and Attack Mode activations. Crucially, unlike earlier regulations where front axle traction was restricted to specific launch or boost phases, permanent all wheel drive is active throughout traction and cornering phases, transforming how drivers rotate the car on tight street circuits and high speed permanent venues alike.

Energy recovery reaches equally staggering territory. The I Type 08 harvests up to 700 kilowatts under regenerative braking, allowing a vast share of total race energy to be generated dynamically during deceleration zones. While the carbon fiber survival cell and enlarged spec battery remain standardized across the grid to contain escalating budgets, Jaguar has brought additional control hardware in house for Season 13, designing bespoke front and rear brake by wire systems alongside its own direct current converter to sharpen pedal feel, thermal management, and software integration.

## Why Vandoorne's development mileage gave him the edge for the race seat

Securing the vacant factory seat alongside da Costa rewards a calculated twelve month investment by Vandoorne after the Belgian missed out on a full time Season 12 race drive. Rather than drifting away from the paddock while competing in endurance racing, the former McLaren Formula 1 driver embedded himself inside Jaguar's Oxfordshire headquarters throughout 2026 as official test and reserve pilot, logging extensive private track mileage in early GEN4 development mules.

Speaking at Tuesday's launch, Vandoorne noted that carrying out heavy development running in the I Type 08 prototype made stepping up to race the finished car feel extra special, adding that he already knows Jaguar's engineering group intimately and is eager to build immediately on the foundations laid behind closed doors. In a radical rule reset where brake by wire calibration, four wheel torque vectoring, and energy targets start from a clean sheet, starting Madrid testing with a driver who helped write the simulator and track baseline eliminates months of adaptation lag.

On the opposite side of the garage, Portuguese racer da Costa enters his second campaign with Jaguar after arriving from Porsche last year. Known across the paddock for surgical racecraft in pack pelotons and fearless Attack Mode overtaking, da Costa provides continuity alongside Vandoorne's meticulous technical feedback, giving team principal James Barclay two proven title closers who understand how to bank points across volatile double header weekends.

## Factory firepower plus Envision and Fortescue customer supply

Jaguar's competitive footprint in Season 13 will extend well beyond its two factory blue and white entries. Continuing its model of multi car data validation, Jaguar confirmed on Tuesday that it will supply its GEN4 I Type 08 powertrain package to both the Paysafe Envision Formula E Team and Fortescue Zero Racing across the 2026 to 2027 world championship.

Fielding six Jaguar powered cars on the grid multiplies real time telemetry during short practice sessions, accelerating software mapping for tire wear, regenerative balance, and energy consumption whenever Formula E visits unfamiliar asphalt. Next week's five day collective test at Circuito de Madrid Jarama from November 16 to November 20 will offer the first stopwatch verdict against rival GEN4 packages from Porsche, Nissan, Andretti, Stellantis, and Lola Yamaha, but on paper Jaguar enters the 600 kilowatt era armed with benchmark continuity and championship pedigree in both cockpits.

## How GEN4 racing technology feeds Jaguar's luxury road car transition

Beyond defending its teams' world championship trophy on street circuits from Sao Paulo to London, Tuesday's Knightsbridge launch carries major strategic weight for Jaguar's wider automotive reinvention. As the Coventry marque transitions toward an ultra luxury all electric road car lineup, Formula E's GEN4 regulations concentrate manufacturer freedom squarely on the exact silicon carbide inverter, motor efficiency, thermal cooling, and regenerative braking software domains that determine real world electric driving range and rapid energy management.

Designing both front and rear brake by wire actuators alongside an in house direct current converter for the I Type 08 gives Jaguar's race and road engineers shared data loops on blending friction braking seamlessly with 700 kilowatts of electrical recuperation. Every fraction of a percent gained in inverter efficiency between Vandoorne and da Costa during a forty five minute E Prix translates directly into usable energy at the rear and front axles without adding battery mass.

With Andretti also unveiling its Season 13 colors on Tuesday and rival factories preparing for next Monday's opening green flag at Madrid Jarama, Season 13 promises ferocious competition from the first lap. By pairing two former Formula E world champions with an all wheel drive package developed steadily by Vandoorne throughout 2026, Jaguar has positioned itself at the front of the grid for electric racing's fastest era yet.
