# Three Arrested After Grenade Attack Kills Woman in Kafr Manda

Three suspects have been arrested in connection with a grenade attack that killed a woman and injured three others in the northern town of Kafr Manda, Israeli police said Saturday. The arrests came as police seized the vehicle believed to have been used in the attack and the country's police chief arrived on the scene to oversee the investigation.

## Arrests and seized vehicle

Police said the three suspects were arrested for being involved in the alleged throwing of a grenade that killed a woman and injured three others in Kafr Manda. Officers also seized the car from which they believe the grenade was thrown, in what appears to be a swift response to the attack.

Israel Police Commissioner Daniel Levy was on the scene in Kafr Manda to receive an assessment of the ongoing investigation. His presence underscored the seriousness with which police are treating the incident.

## Investigation ongoing

The investigation into the grenade attack remains ongoing, with police working to establish the full circumstances of the incident and the roles of those arrested. Further details on the victims and the motive behind the attack have not yet been released.
