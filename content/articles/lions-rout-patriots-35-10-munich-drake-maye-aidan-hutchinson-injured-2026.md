The Detroit Lions cruised to a 35-10 win over the New England Patriots in Munich on Sunday, but the scoreline will matter far less to both teams than one play in the first quarter.

On New England's opening drive, Lions pass rusher Aidan Hutchinson got past Patriots left tackle Will Campbell on third-and-goal and brought down quarterback Drake Maye. Maye came up hurting his right ankle. Hutchinson jumped to celebrate, and his right knee buckled as he landed.

Neither star came back. Maye was ruled out in the second quarter. Hutchinson was listed as questionable and stayed on the sideline for the rest of the afternoon.

## Update: Maye could miss the rest of the season

*Updated Monday, November 16.* The news on Drake Maye has turned worse. Maye is believed to have suffered a significant lower leg injury that will keep him out for an extended period, and it is possible he will miss the rest of the season, NFL Network's Ian Rapoport reported on Monday. Maye has returned from Germany and will seek more medical information.

A team source told The Athletic that Maye has a serious ankle injury and is expected to miss extended time. He was set for more tests on Monday to get a clearer timeline.

Before Sunday, Maye had made 43 straight starts and had never missed a regular-season start because of injury. The Patriots, now 5-5, have a bye this week and next play the Los Angeles Chargers on November 29. Tommy DeVito, who completed 12 of 24 passes for 81 yards with a touchdown and an interception after replacing Maye, is in line to start if Maye is out.

Officials ruled the sack did not meet the standard for a flag under the NFL's hip-drop tackle ban. "I don't think it was anything intentional," Patriots coach Mike Vrabel said. "That's part of the game."

## One sack, two injuries

The Patriots had moved the ball well to start, going 70 yards in 11 plays to get inside the Detroit 10. Maye completed three of his four passes for 48 yards and ran once for 11 yards on the drive.

Then Hutchinson arrived. Replays suggested the sack may have involved a hip-drop tackle, the move the NFL banned in 2024, with Hutchinson's weight landing on Maye's right leg. The Patriots settled for an Andy Borregales 33-yard field goal and a 3-0 lead, their only lead of the game.

TV pictures showed Maye doubled over on the sideline next to head coach Mike Vrabel. He was officially ruled out early in the second quarter.

Hutchinson's injury carries extra weight in Detroit. He broke his left tibia and fibula in October 2024, an injury that ended his season, and he broke his right ankle while at Michigan in 2020. Neither team had given an update on severity by the end of the game.

## Gibbs and St. Brown take over

Detroit answered almost immediately. Jared Goff found Amon-Ra St. Brown for a 56-yard touchdown midway through the first quarter, and the Lions never trailed again.

Goff hit Isaac TeSlaa for a 12-yard score early in the second quarter, and Jahmyr Gibbs ran in from 5 yards out before halftime to make it 21-3 at the break.

St. Brown caught his second touchdown, from 28 yards, in the third quarter. After New England's only touchdown, a 10-yard pass from Tommy DeVito to Lan Larison, Gibbs put the game away with a 28-yard run in the fourth quarter.

Gibbs finished with 153 rushing yards on 22 carries and two touchdowns, plus seven catches for 99 yards. That is more than 250 yards from scrimmage on a day the Lions played without their best defensive player for most of it.

Goff completed 20 of 31 passes for 303 yards and three touchdowns. He also threw two interceptions, one to Kevin Byard and one to Christian Gonzalez. St. Brown had four catches for 98 yards, and Jameson Williams added 50 receiving yards.

## DeVito struggles in relief

DeVito found it hard going against a Detroit pass rush that kept coming even without Hutchinson. He completed 12 of 24 passes for 81 yards and was sacked four times. Lions cornerback Roger McCreary intercepted him.

New England managed just 25 yards from Corey Kiner on 10 carries. Mack Hollins was the leading receiver with five catches for 48 yards.

## What it means

Detroit moves to 7-2 and stays among the NFC's leading teams, though the Lions' next few weeks may depend on what the scans show on Hutchinson's knee.

New England falls to 5-5. The Patriots' bye week comes at a useful time, giving Maye extra days to recover before their next game. If he cannot play, Vrabel will have to choose between DeVito and rookie Behren Morton.

The game was the NFL's latest regular-season game in Germany and drew 70,225 fans to Bayern Munich's stadium.
