President Donald Trump appeared to doze off on Thursday while Chinese President Xi Jinping addressed world leaders at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit in Shenzhen, China, a moment that spread quickly online just hours before Trump's historic meeting with Xi and Russian President Vladimir Putin.

When the live broadcast of the APEC leaders' meeting cut to Trump, he was seated with his head slightly lowered and his eyes apparently closed, with a microphone and meeting papers in front of him, Hong Kong outlet HK01 reported. Journalists in the summit's media center immediately pulled out their phones and cameras to capture the scene on the big screen.

A short close-up clip shared by the BBC shows Trump sitting behind a desk while Xi speaks in Chinese. His head moves slightly before his eyes open again. Other leaders in the room included Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney and Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese.

## Was he asleep?

It is not clear. Other clips from the same day show Trump speaking clearly on several issues, and Hindustan Times noted it is unclear whether his eyes were fully closed or whether the camera angle made it look that way.

That did not stop the clip from going viral. California Gov. Gavin Newsom's press office shared it on X with the comment: "The world is laughing at him."

## A packed schedule

Trump, 80, has had a demanding few days. He arrived in Manila, the Philippine capital, on Tuesday evening for a regional summit, then landed in Shenzhen on Wednesday afternoon, where Xi greeted him with [a rare hug on the tarmac](https://smartaiintel.com/articles/trump-putin-xi-trilateral-summit-china-2026/). He attended a welcome banquet that evening, and his motorcade left his hotel for the APEC venues at about 9:24 a.m. Thursday, according to the South China Morning Post.

Later in the same morning session, Trump spoke out against regulating artificial intelligence. "We oppose any form of globalist regulation or anything to control and stifle AI, and we won't allow that to happen, frankly," he said.

## Not the first time

Trump has faced similar claims before, including allegations that he has fallen asleep during meetings in the Oval Office.

In June, Secretary of State Marco Rubio told the House Foreign Affairs Committee: "I've never seen him fall asleep. On the contrary, the guy doesn't sleep, which is a big problem," adding that Trump had called him in the early hours of the morning.

Some images of Trump apparently asleep have been fake. In April, AFP reported that a viral picture of Trump sleeping at a White House event was a parody made by a graphic artist.

## The big meeting

The moment came on the biggest day of Trump's Asia trip. After the morning session, Trump held separate talks with Xi and with Putin, according to pool reports and Chinese state media, ahead of the first-ever trilateral meeting of the US, Chinese and Russian leaders. Trump has said he wants to discuss limiting nuclear weapons and ending the war in Ukraine.

"They both like Trump," he told Fox News on Thursday. "Now, what happens after me? That I can't tell you."
