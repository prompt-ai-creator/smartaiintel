Victor Wembanyama delivered one of the most jaw dropping two way stat lines in modern NBA history early Tuesday Eastern time, pouring in 40 points, grabbing 11 rebounds, and swatting 9 shots in just 25 minutes on 15 of 19 shooting as the San Antonio Spurs dismantled the Phoenix Suns 122 to 96 in Phoenix for their ninth consecutive victory. According to the [official CBS Sports and Associated Press game recap](https://www.cbssports.com/nba/gametracker/recap/NBA_20261109_SA@PHO/), the 7 foot 4 French phenomenon came within a single blocked shot of a three quarter triple double before spending the entire final period resting on the visitors' bench with the blowout long decided.

Improving to 9 and 2 overall and a spotless 5 and 0 away from home, San Antonio continues pacing a blistering top tier in the Western Conference alongside [Golden State's own surging eight game winning streak](https://smartaiintel.com/articles/stephen-curry-passes-derozan-warriors-rout-pelicans-streak/). Even more daunting for rival coaches trying to devise single coverage or double team answers is Wembanyama's escalating offensive efficiency: Tuesday morning's clinic arrived immediately on the heels of his towering 52 point, 24 rebound demolition of the Dallas Mavericks, giving the 22 year old centerpiece 92 points and 35 rebounds across his past two outings.

Phoenix star guard Devin Booker scored 32 points to try to keep the hosts afloat, and rookie center Khaman Maluach chipped in 11 points, yet the Suns never recovered once Wembanyama erased the paint at one end and rained jumpers over outstretched contests at the other.

## Quarter by quarter dominance and a near triple double in 25 minutes

San Antonio dictated terms from the opening tip, winning all four quarters by counts of 31 to 25, 30 to 21, 32 to 22, and 29 to 28 according to the [official game box score](https://www.cbssports.com/nba/gametracker/boxscore/NBA_20261109_SA@PHO/). By halftime, Wembanyama had already piled up 22 points, 6 rebounds, and 5 blocked shots, turning Phoenix drives into hesitant pull ups or outright retreats back toward the perimeter arc.

Rather than settling exclusively for trail three pointers, Wembanyama punished smaller Phoenix switch defenders on deep seals inside the restricted area while pulling big men twenty six feet from the basket with effortless catch and shoot touch. Making 15 of 19 field goal attempts translated to a blistering 78.9 percent clip from the floor, allowing San Antonio to pile up 93 points through three quarters and stretch its cushion to 25 entering the fourth.

Just as devastating was Wembanyama's rim deterrence. Several of his 9 blocks came on weakside rotations where Phoenix ball handlers believed they had turned the corner cleanly, only for Wembanyama's eight foot wingspan to pin layup attempts against the glass and ignite instant San Antonio fast breaks the other way. Had head coach Mitch Johnson's staff needed him for even two possessions of the fourth quarter, Wembanyama almost certainly would have recorded one of the fastest 40 point triple doubles with blocks ever logged since the NBA began tracking rejections in 1973.

## Dylan Harper and De'Aaron Fox unlock effortless spacing around San Antonio's star

What separates this 9 and 2 San Antonio juggernaut from earlier rebuilding iterations is the dynamic backcourt creation flanking Wembanyama on every possession. Guard Dylan Harper supplied 17 crisp points off slashes and secondary pick and roll reads, while veteran speedster De'Aaron Fox added 15 points and relentless paint touches that prevented Phoenix from loading two defenders onto Wembanyama's hip before catches.

Having both Fox's elite end to end burst and Harper's poised playmaking alongside Wembanyama has transformed San Antonio's half court geometry. Whenever opposing centers step up to level Fox at the nail on high ball screens, Wembanyama slips behind coverage for uncontested lobs; whenever defenders stay attached to Wembanyama's pop above the break, Fox and Harper attack unguarded lanes straight to the rim.

Defensively, perimeter length across San Antonio's guard and wing rotation funneled Booker and Phoenix's secondary handlers directly toward Wembanyama's patrol zone, holding the Suns to 46 first half points and 22 in the third quarter before garbage time benches emptied.

## Early MVP and Defensive Player of the Year momentum in the West

Eleven games into the 2026 to 2027 regular season, Wembanyama is mounting a simultaneous case for both NBA Most Valuable Player and Defensive Player of the Year honors while keeping his minutes load remarkably light. Logging only 25 minutes in Phoenix preserves fresh legs across autumn travel stretches while still delivering historic per minute production that warps every opponent's scouting report.

For Phoenix, absorbing a 26 point home loss despite 32 points from Booker underscores how wide the interior gap remains against the Western Conference's elite length, even as young seven footer Maluach gains valuable early career reps against the league's premier big men. For undefeated road warriors San Antonio, nine straight wins and back to back 52 point and 40 point masterpieces from their franchise cornerstone have announced beyond doubt that the Spurs' championship window is wide open right now.

## Why San Antonio's unbeaten road start signals sustainable playoff pedigree

Blowing out division and conference foes away from Frost Bank Center has been the clearest indicator that San Antonio's 9 and 2 start represents genuine contender substance rather than early schedule fortune. Winning five straight road games requires defensive travel habits, low turnover discipline, and half court shot creation that holds up even when role players endure brief shooting lulls in hostile arenas.

Against Phoenix, San Antonio never allowed Devin Booker's 32 point scoring outbursts to trigger extended Suns runs because Mitch Johnson's rotations maintained relentless ball pressure and defensive rebounding discipline whenever Wembanyama checked out for scheduled breathers. Rather than bleeding double digit swings during non Wembanyama minutes as young San Antonio squads occasionally did two seasons ago, Fox and Harper kept offensive pace brisk and forced Phoenix to defend multiple paint touches on every trip.

Resting their 7 foot 4 franchise cornerstone for the entire fourth quarter of a road rout also pays cumulative dividends across an eighty two game marathon. While rival Western Conference stars log thirty eight high stress minutes to scrape out late clutch victories, Wembanyama walked off the Phoenix floor having needed barely half an hour of real time court work to compile 40 points, 11 boards, and 9 blocks. If San Antonio maintains anywhere near this blend of perimeter speed, interior shot blocking, and historic shooting efficiency, catching the Spurs atop the West will require near perfection.
